💡 The Happy Takeaway: In multicast, the NIC (Network Interface Card) filters packets using the Multicast MAC address (01-00-5e-...) before they ever reach the CPU. Only subscribed hosts process the packet up the network stack (IP → UDP → App). Non-interested hosts drop the packet at the hardware layer—literally zero CPU cycles wasted. This is why your server doesn't spike to 100% when streaming video or handling discovery protocols!
