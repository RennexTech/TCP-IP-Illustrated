# Networking & TCP/IP Stuff (Yes… it’s a lot)

Welcome to possibly one of the **most dense networking repositories on the internet.**

This repo contains my notes from a variety of **Networking and TCP/IP books**.

I spent months reading, summarizing, and occasionally questioning my life choices while building this.  
It was difficult, long, and at several points I wanted to quit in the middle.

<img width="365" height="275" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/32a38435-dba4-4953-8b2c-7a06e6af7908" />

## Why is this free?

Because **knowledge hoarded is knowledge lost.**

Also… because networking already hurts enough.

---

## Who is this for?

- People who enjoy networking, whether **physically working with hardware** or **digitally experimenting with Packet Tracer** (CCNA / networking nerds).
- People who opened **Wireshark once and never fully recovered.**
- Developers interested in **reverse engineering**, **malware analysis**, and **network internals**.

---

## The Best Thing?

This repository is **slightly over-engineered.**

What do I mean by that?

It caters to **all types of computer nerds**:

- Those working with **network software programming**
- Those dealing with **hardware configurations**
- And everyone in between

That means you can **read what you want and skip the rest.**

---

## Structure

To make navigation easier, the content is organized into:

- **Chapters**
- **Table of Contents**
- A companion **Google Site**

So you don’t have to suffer trying to find a specific subtopic.

---

## Final Words

If you made it here, you're probably the kind of person who enjoys this sort of thing.

So grab some coffee ☕, open the notes, and have a blast.
