Solution: Full utilization of 400Gbps NICs

🧠 Why it's a Game Changer

In traditional networking, the CPU has to "touch" every 64KB packet header. At speeds like 100Gbps, the CPU spends 100% of its time just processing headers, leaving no room for actual applications. BIG TCP uses the IPv6 Hop-by-Hop Options header to carry Jumbograms, allowing the system to process one massive block of data instead of thousands of tiny ones.
