Confusion Clearing: BIC vs. BIG

Two very similar acronyms, two completely different layers of the networking stack.

📉 BIC-TCP
Binary Increase Congestion Control
The Core Concept
A congestion control algorithm designed for high-speed networks with high latency (High BDP). It is the predecessor to CUBIC.
How it works
Uses a Binary Search strategy to find the available bandwidth. It aggressively increases the window when far from saturation and slows down as it approaches the limit.
Primary Goal
Window Management. To grow cwnd (Congestion Window) fast enough to fill a 10Gbps pipe without causing massive packet loss.

🚛 BIG TCP
Packet Batching Optimization
The Core Concept
An OS Kernel/Driver optimization (specifically Linux) that allows the networking stack to process "mega-packets" larger than 64KB.
How it works
It enables IPv6 headers to carry Jumbogram information internally, allowing TSO/GRO to handle batches up to 192KB+ per interrupt.
Primary Goal
CPU Efficiency. Reducing the number of CPU interrupts required to push 100Gbps traffic by grouping packets into massive chunks.
